Laurier STEM Publishing Society (LSPS) is made of many science and publishing passionate Waterloo campus students!

About Us

Our objective is to promote a welcoming environment for students in STEM that are interested in publishing in any field by discussing publication processes.

Our Mission

We want to provide support for students by having monthly discussion portals to contribute to each other's publishing goals and editorial support from publishing companies and experienced individuals.

We hope to work with other clubs of aligned interest and bring in guest speakers in the writing industry to build connections. Most importantly, we look forward to making life-long relationships with like-minded people! 🔬🥼💻📈💡
